KANSALAISTREENI — TRANSLATION REVIEW

Thank you for helping! Kansalaistreeni is a free Android app without ads for practising basic facts about Finnish society, for example before the citizenship test. It is unofficial: it is not a government service. The questions are always in Finnish or Swedish, as in the test. Your language helps learners understand the explanations, words and menus.

These translations were made by machine translation, and no native speaker has checked them yet. The app tells learners this until a person has checked the text.

WHAT TO DO
1. Open 1-study-content.csv in Excel or Google Sheets ({CONTENT} rows). Each row shows the Finnish original, the English version and the translation in your language. Rows are grouped by topic, so you can do one topic at a time or share topics with a friend.
2. Read the translation. Is it correct, natural and easy to understand (simple everyday language, about B1)? Does it say the same as the Finnish text?
3. In the column "OK / Fix" write OK, or write Fix and put your corrected text or a comment in the last column.
4. Do the same for 2-app-interface.csv ({INTERFACE} menu and button texts) and read 3-store-listing.txt (the app's description in Google Play). Keep codes such as %1$s and %d unchanged: the app replaces them with numbers or words.
5. For official names, use the words in 4-glossary.csv. Where the glossary says so, keep the Finnish word in brackets after the translation, because learners will see the Finnish word in the test.

You do not need to check the Finnish facts, but if something looks wrong, add a comment. You can stop at any time and send what you have done: even one topic helps.

WHEN YOU ARE DONE
Send the files back to {CONTACT}. Please tell us whether we may mention your name as the reviewer. The project is open source, so the name would be public. If you prefer, we write only "native-speaker reviewer".
