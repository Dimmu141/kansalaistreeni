## Recording a decision

For each pair, add a dated record with: question IDs/fingerprints; reviewer and role; exact evidence and source date; factual decision; FI/SV/EN/easier-Finnish decisions; reuse decision; proposed correction with reason; and publication decision. Do not mark approval merely because machine validation passes. Store only reviewer information they agree to share.

Corrections follow [CONTENT-AUTHORING.md](CONTENT-AUTHORING.md): increment package version; increment question revision when practised content changes; translation-only edits may retain the revision. Refresh source-review fingerprints after actual review and run the complete verification gate. Review the changed reading lesson, vocabulary and backup/restore journey as well as the question itself.

Official source links do not turn app-authored questions into official exam questions. Keep DEMO status until a separate editorial/publication decision supports a change. Authentic official material requires reviewed origin and reuse rights.
