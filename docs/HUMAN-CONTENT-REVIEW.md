## Recording a decision

For each pair, add a dated record with: question IDs/fingerprints; reviewer and role; exact evidence and source date; factual decision; FI/SV/EN/easier-Finnish decisions; reuse decision; proposed correction with reason; and publication decision. Do not mark approval merely because machine validation passes. Store only reviewer information they agree to share.

Corrections follow [CONTENT-AUTHORING.md](CONTENT-AUTHORING.md): increment package version; increment question revision when practised content changes; translation-only edits may retain the revision.
